# 🎬 Movie Industry Analysis: From Data to Blockbuster

> Turning movie data into box office insights - My latest creation at Innomatics Research Labs, Dilsukhnagar

This is not just another dashboard. It's a **UNIQUE PROJECT** that answers - What makes a movie a hit, who makes it work, and what audience really wants.

### 📊 Dashboards Overview

#### 1. 🎥 Cinema Insights
- Genre-wise & Year-wise movie trends
- Industry patterns and movie count analysis
- What type of movies are dominating?

#### 2. ⭐ Movie Reviews and Performance
- Ratings, Reviews & IMDb analysis
- Critics vs Audience score comparison
- Which movies performed best?

#### 3. 💰 Movie Revenue and Director Insights
- Director vs Box Office revenue analysis
- Budget vs Profit - ROI decoding
- Who is the most bankable director?

#### 4. 👥 Audience & Popularity Insights
- Audience scores and popularity trends
- What drives viewership and fan following?
- Social buzz & popularity drivers

### 🔍 Key Insights
- Genre and Director directly impact Box Office collection
- High budget doesn't always mean high profit - Story matters
- Audience rating is the real king for long-term popularity
- Specific directors consistently deliver higher ROI

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Power BI** - 4 Interactive Dashboards
- **Python** - Data Cleaning & EDA
- **DAX** - Advanced Calculations
- **Dataset** - Movies Dataset (IMDb / Box Office)

### 🚀 How to Use
1. Clone the repo
2. Open Power BI file (.pbix)
3. Explore 4 dashboards
4. Check insights
